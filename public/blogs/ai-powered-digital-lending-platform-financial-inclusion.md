## Why Choose Digital Lending Platforms?
When asked why banks are treating digital lending as a priority, McKinsey Partner Gerald Chappell gives three key reasons:
1.  **Huge gains in terms of customer experience.**
2.  **The ability to make faster and better quality decisions.**
3.  **The massive cost savings that come from running credit operations digitally.**

This approach has seen rapid growth, fundamentally transforming how people borrow and lend money. As a result, the fintech lending market is projected to surpass $400 billion by 2028, with an expected annual growth rate of 18%—three times faster than the 6% growth rate of traditional banking, according to a [study by McKinsey & Company](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services).
